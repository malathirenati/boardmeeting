Edit mode is a preview: try changes here to see how they will look. To keep them, copy your tab's rows into the meeting sheet, then press Sync.
  1. Press Copy rows.
  2. Open the meeting sheet (Edit in Google Sheets ↗) and go to this circle's tab.
  3. Select columns A to J from row 5 down and delete their contents, click cell A5 and paste.
  4. Pictures added in Edit mode are not copied: insert each into its Picture row's column D (Insert → Image → Image in cell).
  5. Press Sync. The dashboard updates in two to three minutes.
